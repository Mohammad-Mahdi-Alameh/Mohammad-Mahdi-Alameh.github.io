01 — About

Building software that just works.

I'm a Full Stack Web Developer based in Bchamoun, Lebanon, with a BSc in Computer Science from the Lebanese University. I love turning complex business logic into clean, intuitive, and maintainable experiences.

At CCC I architect and scale enterprise Angular platforms (up to v21) for Talisman Procurement, Awards Expenditure and HRMS — using standalone components and lazy loading to squeeze out every bit of performance. I lean on advanced TypeScript OOP, design patterns, RxJS and Signals for reactive state, and I secure apps with OAuth2 interceptors and role-based route guards.
